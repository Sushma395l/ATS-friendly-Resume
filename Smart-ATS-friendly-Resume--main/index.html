Understand

What is an Applicant Tracking System (ATS)?

An ATS is software used by employers to collect, scan, sort, and rank job applications. Over 98% of Fortune 500 companies use an ATS to screen resumes before they reach a human recruiter.

Parser Failures

Tables, floating text box columns, and graphs block standard ATS tools from parsing. They see blank pages or scrambled characters, leading to automatic rejection.

Keyword Matching

The systems search for specific skills, technologies, and certifications from the job description. Matching keyword density directly determines your candidate rank.
